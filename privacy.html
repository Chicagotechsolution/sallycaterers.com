Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sally Caterers' website is currently a static informational site. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

This site does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising scripts. The date-hold form does not transmit data anywhere before bookings open; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Hosting

The site is served by our hosting provider, which may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.

At launch

When bookings open, this policy will be replaced with one describing exactly what enquiry and booking data is collected and how it is used, before any collection begins.
