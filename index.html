What we cater

Three things, done properly.

Weddings

Plated, stations, or family style · 50–400 guests

Your menu, tasted and settled months ahead, then executed without drama on the day. We handle staffing, rentals coordination, and the cake-cutting logistics nobody thinks about until 9 p.m.

Corporate

Lunches, launches, holiday parties · 15–250

Hot spreads and boxed lunches that show up early, labeled for every diet in the building. Standing weekly orders get their own menu rotation so Tuesday never tastes like last Tuesday.

BBQ

Backyards, graduations, company picnics · 30–500

Brisket and pork shoulder smoked overnight, chicken for the sensible, and sides made from somebody's grandmother's recipe — because they are. On-site smoker service for crowds that want the show.
